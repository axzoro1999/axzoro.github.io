I am a robotics researcher at **ByteDance Seed-Robotics**, working on
vision-language-action models that turn multi-modal observations into reliable
real-world manipulation behaviour.

My research asks how much of a robot's competence can come from data that is
cheap to collect. That leads me to latent action paradigms, scalable and
data-efficient representation learning, and world cognition — learning the
dynamics of physical interaction well enough that a policy can generalise
beyond the demonstrations it was trained on.

Feel free to reach out if you would like to talk about manipulation, embodied
AI, or a possible collaboration.
